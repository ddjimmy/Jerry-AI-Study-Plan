# Week 6 — PyTorch Fundamentals

[中文版本](../zh-CN/week06.md)

## Goal
Understand a neural-network training loop.

## Day 1
Learn tensors:
- shape
- dtype
- device
- scalar/vector/matrix/tensor

## Day 2
Implement and experiment with `y = wx + b`.

## Day 3
Understand loss by measuring prediction error.

## Day 4
Learn:
- gradient
- backpropagation
- optimizer

Be able to explain:
- `optimizer.zero_grad()`
- `loss.backward()`
- `optimizer.step()`

## Day 5
Train a simple neural network on FashionMNIST or a similarly simple dataset.

### Weekly Demo
Walk through one complete training iteration in your own words.
